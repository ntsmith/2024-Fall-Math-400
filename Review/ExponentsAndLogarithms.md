# Review: Exponents and Logarithms

## Exponential Function

We generally consider $f(x)=b^x$, where $b > 0$ and $x$ is arbitrary.
The domain is all of $\mathbb R$ and the range is $(0,\infty)$.

## Rules of Exponents

$a^m b^n = a^{m+n}$

$(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$

$(a b)^n = a^n b^n$

$\frac{a^n}{a^m} = a^{n-m}$

$a^{\frac{1}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a}$

$a^0 = 1$

## Log functions

$\log_b(x)$ is defined to be the inverse of $b^x$

$y = \log_b(x)$ means that $x = b^y$

## Rule of Logs

$\log_b(b^x) = x$

$b^{\log_b x} = x$

$\log_b(x y) = \log_b(x) + \log_b(y)$

$\log_b(\frac x y) = \log_b(x) + \log_b(y)$

$\log_b(b) = 1$

$\log_b(1) = 0$
